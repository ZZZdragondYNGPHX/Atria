# Storage management

Open the storage center from Atria's workspace. Switch between **Server account** and **Current browser** to inspect each scope separately.

- **Overview** groups resources by their current owner and shows usage.
- **Cleanup suggestions** lists data that the relevant owner has verified can be cleaned.
- **Backups & recycle bin** shows recoverable items and supported recovery actions.
- **Operation history** shows cleanup and recovery tasks.
- **Advanced tools** exposes the raw storage inspector for older or unclassified files.

Available actions depend on the resource and its references. Review a preview before applying a cleanup; resources in use or needed for recovery may be protected.

The raw inspector's **Other** category includes the `assets` directory, which can contain active Native Work packages and media. Its size alone does not mean those files are obsolete or safe to delete. Manage Works and their dependencies through the owning Library or storage-center actions.

For account archives and transfer, see [Backup and transfer](/guide/migration).
