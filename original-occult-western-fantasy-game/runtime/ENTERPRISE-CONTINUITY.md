# Progression, Wealth and Delegated Institutions — Phase 5

Status: implemented Phase 5 candidate, not the final Long-Lived World release.
Development version: 2.0.0-phase5. Exact verified commits/CI belong to the same
Package Record and live HANDOFF on docs.

## Ownership and entry points

The default declaration pipeline adds enterprise after renewal. The optional
Native lifetimes.enterprise policy initializes state under atri_lifecycle.lifetimes.enterprise.
All writes use the existing Lifetime candidate, Native clock, CAS publication,
History and SaveSystem. No alternate scheduler, RNG service or persistence exists.

The opening.wait lifetime input supports progress.learn and identity.change plus
four grouped commands: career.change, asset.change, organization.change and
delegate.change. Their verb selects the concrete action. Typed callers can use
enterpriseCommand from public/shared/native-enterprise-contract.js; this builds
validated input only. Grouping and balanced declaration formulas retain the existing
24-command and expression-depth limits. Nested prohibited flags use typed scalar
formula leaves, not an object-valued formula shortcut.

## Progression, credentials and public identity

Investigation, commerce and occult ranks mature at 3 in this Package. A resolved
canonical Matter is required for learning; the same domain/source cannot be reused.
Later cases add persistent source-linked knowledge rather than endless ranks.
Occult learning also raises Claim burden and exposure. Reconstruction clears
embodied growth, not knowledge, old obligations or canonical history.

Roles are evidence-backed permission/obligation bundles, not lifetime classes.
Investigation, trade, administration and research gate their corresponding actions;
institution-required roles require a live institution. Suspended credentials also
remove the actor's obsolete career. Leaving a necessary role suspends delegation.
Obligation text describes the role; this is not a general legal-code interpreter.

One persistent protagonist may retain, hide, retire, declare dead or replace its
public identity. Replacement has a 40-unit registration cost; forged continuity
adds traceable liabilities and exposure. The old identity and family/court link
history remain. Licenses suspend, active delegations escalate and titles/banking
become inaccessible rather than silently following a new name. Bank and title
regularization each cost 20 and increase exposure. The older Package continuity
record is a synchronized projection, checked after Ready, not another authority.

## Property, money and provenance

Property binds to existing locations, businesses or districts. Each place has at
most one title; purchase has a declared price, financial movement and immutable
ownership event. Capitalization, withdrawal, repair, transfer, named bequest and
legal regularization preserve provenance and nonnegative account balances.
The ledger reconciles opening reserves plus all movements to current balances.

Death/absence triggers causal inheritance or an estate. Reconstruction cannot
reclaim property already inherited by another person. Dissolved institutional
owners become estates; burned/demolished places destroy their bound property.
Delegated operations pay taxes/maintenance, earn trade income, degrade condition
and may incur real debt, seizure or closure. A closed business is unavailable for
new live Matters. An already-open Matter can use attributed archival evidence and
record a conclusion, but cannot pretend to settle at a destroyed/closed venue.

This is a bounded local enterprise abstraction, not a market-price simulator,
macro economy, bank lending system or arbitrary trust/share instrument language.
Routine business cashflow is resolved through an explicit delegation contract.

## Delegation and institutional autonomy

A contract names an adult active actor OR an existing institutional office, an
objective, target, resource ceiling, risk tolerance, prohibited action flags,
mandatory occult escalation, loss threshold and a 1–10 year reporting cadence.
Ordinary investigations receive a seven-day/deadline-bounded review instead.

Business results depend on the actual actor's competence/health, instruction
specificity, capital, local pressure and institutional agenda. Authority excess
pauses before spending. Corruption can divert real money and conceal reports;
a paid audit reveals existing incidents without rewriting past balances.
Betrayal, insolvency and organizational resistance change persistent world state.
No church-contract, coercive-action or voluntary borrowing action is implemented;
prohibitions are not permission to invent such off-contract activity. Misconduct
may breach policy and become discoverable rather than being magically prevented.

Routine investigations either execute the existing evidence path and conclude,
close unresolved with real pressure consequences, or escalate personal/occult/Hook
risk. Restricted research remains bounded; occult decisions return to the player.
Practical family logistics cannot substitute for presence: long absence may
estrange a bond, while explicit consensual reconciliation starts a fresh interval.

Player-founded institutions may receive charters and up to four levels of offices
and aggregate workforce. Named leadership uses existing Native succession;
workforce size does not materialize hundreds of actors. Office contracts follow
real successors; named contracts cannot resurrect dead/retired actors. Dissolved
institutions do not refill closed offices. Repeated organizational reviews may
shift agenda toward leadership interests and ultimately refuse founder orders.
City-scale influence is an optional workforce/institution projection, not conquest.

## Clock, privacy and retention

Due reviews join the existing sparse Lifetime event queue; lifecycle exits at the
same instant run before enterprise work. Fixed review anniversaries make whole
and split advances equivalent. Escalation pauses the contract for player authority;
it does not invent a second clock or globally freeze the world.

enterpriseView is a bounded cloned backend projection of public identity,
credentials, titles, reports and organizational state. It does not reveal hidden
loyalty, concealed incidents or true unreported balances. Raw assets/contracts are
private History sources; audits publish explicitly discovered facts. This helper
is not yet a frontend sandbox binding. Final UI is Phase 7.

The new record ceiling is 256, within existing Lifetime/History byte and event
budgets. Exceeding any budget rejects atomically; no fact is dropped and no global
limit is raised. This finite stage does not prove unlimited enterprise-history
growth. Portable exports use the existing history.compact; explicit old SavePoints
and checkpoint/Retry dependency boundaries remain protected.

## Verification

Run node tools/package.mjs validate --enterprise-only --core <compatible-core>.
The focused candidate spans 50 years with 20 years/four routine delegated reviews,
eight real alternating Fs/SQLite Save/Restore transitions, identity/title repair,
organizational resistance, inheritance/reconstruction and retained marked evidence.
Compaction does not count as a meaningful turn. This is NOT Gate A, B or C and does
not rerun or replace the prior 5k/50-year renewable-content candidate.

Native tests also cover seed matrices, split intervals and JSON normalization,
concealed failure/audit, succession, actual venue closure, budget rejection,
pre-Ready identity validation and old SavePoints/checkpoint/Retry across storage
adapters. Only the exact executed checks and CI results in the Record are evidence.

No multi-region, Era/macro, final UI, v1 save migration or final 2.0.0 artifact is
included. releases/1.0.0.atria remains unchanged.
