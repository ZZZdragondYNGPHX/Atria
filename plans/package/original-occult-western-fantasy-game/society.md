### 6.14 Approved social structure — Round 3

Society is derived from the approved metaphysics rather than designed as an unrelated fantasy backdrop.

### 6.14.1 Public supernatural, restricted mechanism

The supernatural is **publicly acknowledged but unevenly understood**.

Ordinary people know that:

- some oaths and offices have real consequences;
- certain places, objects and events can retain abnormal residue;
- churches, courts and state registries perform procedures with more than symbolic force;
- licensed investigators and practitioners exist;
- some phenomena cannot be explained through ordinary science alone.

However, most people do not know formal Claim engineering, complete Anchor theory, the Common Eight in technical detail, or anything reliable about Layer C.

The social knowledge ladder is therefore:

1. public custom and folklore;
2. professional procedure;
3. licensed occult engineering;
4. restricted high theory;
5. unresolved deep cosmology.

### 6.14.2 Modernity as reality stabilization

Industrial and bureaucratic modernity is itself a supernatural transformation.

Population registries, standardized names, mapped property, courts, synchronized time, railways, telegraph networks, newspapers, hospitals, graveyards, police procedure and mass archives create unprecedented networks of mutually reinforcing Anchors.

Modern states therefore stabilize reality more strongly than earlier societies.

This explains why older periods can feel more mythic without relying on a generic “magic used to be stronger” premise: older reality was often less densely recorded, synchronized and cross-verified.

### 6.14.3 Census and registry as supernatural infrastructure

Population census, birth registration, death registration, residence, legal identity and property records are approved as major forms of civic supernatural infrastructure.

They do not automatically dictate truth, but they strongly stabilize Name, Witness and Bond relationships when supported by repeated institutional practice and independent evidence.

The state is therefore becoming one of history's largest reality-stabilization systems.

### 6.14.4 Four major reality authorities

The setting is provisionally structured around four forms of social authority, not four monolithic factions.

#### State / Crown

Primary domains:

- legal Name;
- public Witness;
- borders and Boundary;
- court judgments;
- archives;
- citizenship;
- land and administrative identity;
- police and military authority.

#### Church

Primary domains:

- Bond;
- sacred / communal Name;
- Witness;
- Memory;
- marriage, kinship and life-cycle rites;
- oath;
- burial;
- communal continuity.

#### Academy

Primary domains:

- formal Common Eight theory;
- experimentation;
- Claim comparison;
- failure analysis;
- Investiture research;
- reproducible occult engineering;
- publication and classification.

#### Capital

Primary domains:

- Bond;
- Possibility;
- legal Name;
- contract;
- banking;
- insurance;
- corporations;
- industrial coordination and investment.

Actual organizations may combine or contest these authorities.

### 6.14.5 Old Anchors versus New Anchors

The principal social transformation of the era is not science versus magic.

It is competition between:

**Old Anchors**

- bloodline;
- inherited title;
- local custom;
- parish memory;
- land;
- dynasty;
- sacred tradition.

and:

**New Anchors**

- registry;
- corporation;
- contract;
- centralized law;
- mass literacy;
- statistical knowledge;
- newspaper;
- telegraph;
- standardized time;
- modern administration.

Both can possess real supernatural force.

Industrialization is therefore also a struggle over what forms of reality society will reinforce.

### 6.14.6 Nobility

Traditional nobility derives supernatural weight primarily through combinations of:

- Name;
- Bond;
- Boundary;
- inherited Memory;
- historical Witness.

A title can be genuinely powerful because it is stabilized through land tenure, genealogy, law, ritual, family memory, subordinates and repeated public recognition.

This does not imply magical blood by default.

Modern bureaucracy, corporate identity and centralized law can erode or rival noble Anchors.

### 6.14.7 Corporation as emergent institutional Identity

A sufficiently large and persistent corporation may accumulate:

- legal Name;
- property;
- contracts;
- employees;
- shareholders;
- offices;
- archives;
- repeated public acts;
- independent continuity across human leadership.

This creates the approved possibility of **institutional Identity**: an organization may become increasingly real as an entity in its own right.

This is not yet a universal property of corporations. It is a setting-level phenomenon to explore.

### 6.14.8 Banking and debt

Banking is a large-scale Bond technology.

Debt can be strongly anchored through contract, signature, collateral, repeated accounting, law and institutional memory.

Unresolved or historically distorted obligations may leave persistent supernatural consequences.

Financial institutions therefore matter metaphysically without reducing debt to a simple curse meter.

### 6.14.9 Insurance and quantified risk

Insurance operates near **Possibility + Bond + Witness**.

The industry attempts to define future uncertainty, measure risk and assign obligations before uncertain events resolve.

A high-level research question in the setting is whether sufficiently rigorous measurement and institutional treatment of risk can itself influence Possibility.

This is an approved worldbuilding direction, not yet a finalized mechanic.

### 6.14.10 Courts

Courts do not manufacture Truth by decree.

They:

- collect Witness;
- formalize evidence;
- establish legal Name and Bond;
- publish judgments;
- create powerful public Anchors.

An incorrect judgment therefore does not erase Truth. It can instead create a dangerous contradiction between strongly anchored institutional Belief and the underlying event.

Wrongful convictions and official historical errors can thus produce supernatural consequences.

### 6.14.11 Dual identity structure

A person may be stabilized through overlapping identity systems.

**Civil Identity**

- state registration;
- legal status;
- property;
- profession;
- court records;
- citizenship.

**Sacred / Communal Identity**

- naming;
- family;
- religious rite;
- marriage;
- burial tradition;
- local recognition;
- oath.

These systems normally reinforce one another but can conflict.

Such conflicts are a major source of political, legal, religious and supernatural stories.

### 6.14.12 University and occult industrialization

The Academy represents a major historical disruption because it converts occult practice from guarded tradition into partially reproducible knowledge.

Modern research can:

- compare Claims across cultures;
- publish repeatable procedures;
- classify Principles;
- collect failure cases;
- challenge church or noble monopolies;
- accelerate technological diffusion.

The age is therefore experiencing **occult industrialization** alongside material industrialization.

### 6.14.13 Police and abnormal investigation

Most police remain ordinary professionals.

They are trained in procedures that stabilize and preserve reality:

- isolate witnesses;
- establish time;
- photograph or catalogue evidence;
- control scene access;
- preserve documents;
- verify identity;
- call licensed abnormal investigators when necessary.

Specialist units handle true supernatural cases.

Their procedural discipline is often as important as supernatural capability.

### 6.14.14 Telegraph, press and public Belief

Mass communication accelerates reality settlement.

Telegraphy, standardized time and newspapers can rapidly create common public Belief around an event.

They cannot overwrite strongly anchored Truth by statement alone.

However, when events are still poorly settled, early mass narratives can become powerful Belief Anchors and increase contradiction if they are wrong.

Misinformation can therefore become metaphysically dangerous without granting newspapers unrestricted reality control.

### 6.14.15 Secret organizations

Effective secret organizations may deliberately minimize stable Name and Bond structures.

Methods may include:

- cellular organization;
- inconsistent naming;
- rotating identities;
- minimal central records;
- compartmentalized knowledge;
- temporary obligations;
- anonymous or unregistered property.

This is not aesthetic secrecy alone. Weak institutional anchoring can be an actual defensive technique.

### 6.14.16 Black markets in reality gaps

Occult black markets may traffic in objects and opportunities such as:

- unregistered identities;
- legal identities of the dead that remain administratively active;
- forgotten names;
- broken archives;
- unresolved contracts;
- ownerless relics;
- obsolete passes that still interact with old Boundaries;
- witnesses to suppressed events;
- records that contradict official history.

The core commodity is often not “magic item” but an exploitable gap between systems of reality stabilization.

### 6.14.17 Round 3 decision

Approved:

- publicly acknowledged supernatural phenomena with restricted professional mechanism;
- modernity as increasing reality stabilization;
- census, registry and bureaucracy as supernatural infrastructure;
- State / Church / Academy / Capital as four major reality authorities;
- old Anchor versus new Anchor as a central historical conflict;
- non-magical-blood default for nobility;
- possible institutional Identity for corporations;
- banking and insurance as natural supernatural institutions;
- courts as strong Belief / legal Anchor engines rather than creators of Truth;
- dual civil and sacred / communal identity;
- occult industrialization through universities;
- procedural abnormal investigation;
- mass media as rapid Belief stabilization rather than wish magic;
- intentionally weakly anchored secret organizations;
- black markets built around reality gaps.
