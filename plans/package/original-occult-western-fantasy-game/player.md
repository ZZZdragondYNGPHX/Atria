### 6.20 Round 4 question — player identity and starting situation

Round 4 must determine:

- who the player is before becoming meaningfully supernatural;
- whether the player begins inside or outside an institution;
- what social class / profession gives access to ordinary city life;
- what makes the player capable of surviving or interpreting the opening contradiction;
- whether the first supernatural step is a Breach, Investiture or delayed choice;
- how much character background is fixed versus player-authored;
- how the opening “second death” case reaches the player;
- how to preserve role-playing freedom without making the protagonist socially empty;
- how to avoid making the player a predestined chosen one;
- which early institutional relationships are guaranteed and which are earned.
