### 6.19 Round 3.9 question — city institutions and faction network

Round 3.9 must convert the approved social structure into a playable institutional network.

It must define:

- ordinary police and specialist abnormal-investigation authority;
- the city's university and occult research relationship to government and Church;
- the local Church hierarchy and specialist offices;
- the dominant insurer / banking institution connected to Eastbank archives;
- the major railway / industrial corporation and its Institutional Identity risk;
- old aristocratic landholders and inherited Anchor interests;
- worker organizations and neighborhood mutual-aid networks;
- press and telegraph institutions;
- underground identity, document and relic markets;
- which institutions cooperate, compete or quietly depend on one another;
- which groups hold different fragments of Eastbank evidence;
- how to prevent any single faction from becoming the obvious “good” or “evil” organization.

Formal organization names and named NPC leadership remain deliberately open until the functional network is approved.
