### 6.26 Round 5 question — core gameplay loop and failure model

Round 5 must determine:

- the smallest repeatable gameplay loop;
- how cases begin, branch and conclude;
- whether evidence is collected as discrete facts, documents, relationships or all three;
- how social investigation differs from document investigation and physical exploration;
- when checks / uncertainty resolution are needed and when actions should simply succeed;
- how Claims interact with mundane methods;
- what combat is for and how often it should occur;
- how injury, arrest, debt, damaged relationships and other failures create further play rather than reload pressure;
- how time advances;
- how institutional Agendas advance while the player acts;
- how downtime supports work, research, relationships and recovery;
- how money matters without becoming a survival grind;
- what constitutes failure of a case if the story continues;
- what information the player may safely miss without dead-ending progression.
