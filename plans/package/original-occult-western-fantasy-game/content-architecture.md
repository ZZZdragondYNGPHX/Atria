### 6.40 Approved content scale and authoring architecture — Round 8

The launch content model prioritizes **authored causal depth plus system-generated surface breadth**.

Content quality is measured primarily by interconnection density and systemic relevance rather than total text volume.

#### 6.40.1 Geographic authored footprint

The launch Package should deeply author the already approved primary region rather than attempting to prewrite an entire nation.

Target footprint:

- the six core city districts;
- a small number of nearby estates / towns / rural locations;
- one important railway corridor.

The preferred initial scale is approximately **25–35 durable important locations**.

Generic streets, shops, inns, warehouses, residences and ordinary civic spaces may be instantiated from district / institutional archetypes when needed.

A generated place that becomes important may be promoted into a persistent Entity.

#### 6.40.2 Institutional roster

The Round 3.9 institutional network is sufficient as the primary launch network.

Do not add fantasy organizations merely to increase faction count.

Deep institutional content should focus on:

- ordinary police;
- specialist abnormal authority;
- University / occult research;
- metropolitan Church;
- insurance / banking conglomerate;
- railway / industrial corporation;
- old-estate network;
- worker / mutual-aid networks;
- press / telegraph;
- Grey Registry.

Institutional richness should come from internal interests, records, people, methods and conflict rather than additional organization names.

#### 6.40.3 Institution content contract

Each major institution should eventually define at least:

- Public Role;
- Real Function;
- Jurisdiction;
- important Records;
- what it knows;
- what it does not know;
- one to three significant Agendas;
- two to four important NPCs where appropriate;
- at least one internal contradiction;
- at least one Eastbank connection where applicable;
- characteristic Claim traditions / Investiture methods;
- what access or capability it can grant the player;
- what obligations it can impose;
- what information or event would materially threaten it.

This is an authoring contract, not a UI template.

#### 6.40.4 NPC scale

Preferred launch scale:

**Tier A — approximately 12–16 Major Actors**

These justify full long-term simulation because they carry substantial combinations of:

- Belief;
- Memory;
- Relation;
- Agenda;
- Obligation;
- institutional Knowledge;
- long-term Identity change.

**Tier B — approximately 30–50 Named Supporting Actors**

These persist with stable identity, limited perspective state and availability.

**Tier C — generated Population**

Ordinary workers, clerks, nurses, officers, passengers, customers and passersby are generated from context and are not pre-enumerated.

A Tier C actor may be promoted when repeated play makes them narratively or mechanically important.

These ranges are scope targets rather than exact mandatory launch counts.

#### 6.40.5 Three content layers for Matters / Cases

**Signature Cases**

Highly authored cases that define the game's identity.

Preferred launch target: approximately **6–8**.

A Signature Case should normally contain:

- authoritative underlying Truth;
- multiple viable investigation paths;
- shared Evidence graph;
- meaningful involvement from multiple institutions;
- several possible Settlements;
- persistent consequences after imperfect resolution;
- thematic or structural connection to the wider city / Eastbank mystery where appropriate.

**Institutional Case Patterns**

Reusable medium-scale professional structures.

Preferred launch target: approximately **10–20 patterns**.

Examples may include:

- identity verification;
- insurance dispute;
- property conflict;
- missing person;
- marriage / kinship record contradiction;
- industrial injury record conflict;
- burial / cemetery identity problem.

They are authored as Case Skeletons rather than fixed linear scripts.

**Emergent Matters**

Generated from live state:

- debt;
- meeting;
- personal conflict;
- expiring license;
- witness departure;
- Agenda consequence;
- Anchor obligation.

These do not require a prewritten quest.

#### 6.40.6 Case Skeleton authoring

Reusable Case content should define causal structure rather than scene sequence.

A Case Skeleton should eventually specify concepts such as:

- Premise;
- Underlying Truth;
- participating Actors / institutions;
- Evidence Graph;
- Initial Beliefs;
- Pressures / natural deadlines;
- classes of valid interventions;
- Settlement Families;
- Re-entry Hooks.

Do not author reusable Cases as:

**Scene 1 → Scene 2 → Scene 3 → Boss / answer**

#### 6.40.7 Anomaly Families

Anomalies should be authored as reusable causal families rather than one-off monster entries.

Preferred launch target: approximately **8–12 Anomaly Families**.

Candidate families include:

- Identity Contradiction;
- Boundary Failure;
- Residual Echo;
- Broken Bond;
- Memory Discontinuity;
- Institutional Identity;
- Possibility Saturation;
- Form Continuity Failure.

Additional families may be added only when they represent materially different system behavior rather than cosmetic theme.

#### 6.40.8 Claim Catalog scope

Avoid a huge launch skill list.

Preferred initial target:

- approximately **16–24 Starter Claim Seeds**;
- approximately **30–40 Established Claim Archetypes**.

Variation should primarily come from:

- Condition;
- Anchor;
- Price;
- Jurisdiction;
- institutional stabilization method;
- Principle support combination.

Do not count every institutional Anchor / Price variant as a completely separate ability unless its mechanical rule actually differs.

#### 6.40.9 Claim primitives

Claim content should reuse validated effect primitives where possible.

Examples:

- detect contradiction;
- preserve Memory;
- identify Relation discontinuity;
- stabilize Boundary;
- perceive Echo;
- protect designation;
- narrow unresolved Possibility;
- maintain Form continuity.

This supports later Claim Engineering without requiring every legal Claim combination to be individually hand-written.

#### 6.40.10 Eastbank Canon is fragmented

Do not create one giant always-loaded Eastbank truth document.

Eastbank Canon should be split by evidence ownership / historical responsibility, such as:

- pre-disaster geography;
- identity-registration experiment;
- disaster-day Events;
- Church burial contradiction;
- insurer / property Settlement;
- railway reconstruction;
- worker household Memory;
- academic anomaly studies;
- official Settlement;
- deeper residual structure.

Tasks load only the fragments relevant to their permitted perspective and current problem.

#### 6.40.11 Layered Eastbank revelation

The long mystery should support at least four interpretive layers:

1. **Administrative Error** — fraud, clerical mistake, identity theft or bad records remain plausible.
2. **Settlement Contradiction** — evidence shows that large-scale institutional contradiction really occurred.
3. **Deliberate Stabilization** — the player learns that major authorities knowingly chose a survivable coherent version.
4. **Deep Cause** — later inquiry approaches the older Boundary / Identity structure beneath Eastbank and the limits of ordinary theory.

Each layer should reinterpret earlier Evidence rather than replace it with an unrelated twist.

#### 6.40.12 Content may remain undiscovered

Authored lore and Evidence need not be consumed in every campaign.

Do not force exposition merely because content was expensive to create.

Hidden Canon primarily exists to keep the world coherent and support different routes.

#### 6.40.13 Replay variation

Replay value should come primarily from divergent interaction with a stable world rather than randomized replacement of core Truth.

Variation sources include:

- Origin;
- Prior Life;
- Personal Anchors;
- first Claim;
- institutional relationships;
- Case ordering;
- missed / acquired Evidence;
- different Settlements;
- failed actions;
- Agenda evolution;
- Public Belief.

Do not default to randomizing the culprit / core historical Truth as the main replay mechanism.

#### 6.40.14 Package content modularization

Content should be split into maintainable resources by concern rather than collected into one `world.json` or always-loaded worldbook.

Conceptual content families include:

- geography;
- institutions;
- actors;
- Case Kits;
- Anomaly Families;
- Claim Catalog;
- historical Canon;
- public documents;
- Origin / background content;
- terminology / narrative style resources.

Exact Package Data resource boundaries are deferred to Round 9.

#### 6.40.15 Authored-versus-generated boundary

**Hard-authored / reviewed**

Must include:

- core World Truth;
- Eastbank history;
- major institutions;
- Tier A NPC core Identity;
- Signature Case Truth;
- Claim Catalog;
- Anomaly rules;
- core religion / politics;
- major Settlement / legal world rules.

**Model-assisted under bounded templates**

May include:

- Tier B secondary characterization;
- ordinary document wording;
- newspaper copy;
- daily dialogue;
- instantiated Institutional Case details;
- incidental but persistent minor locations.

**Freely generative within Canon constraints**

May include:

- Tier C population;
- atmosphere;
- ordinary street detail;
- non-canonical shop / crowd texture;
- cosmetic variation without world authority.

Generated content that becomes mechanically important must be promoted into authoritative state.

#### 6.40.16 Interconnection density over word count

Content scope should be judged by how many meaningful systemic connections an authored element supports.

A strong NPC may simultaneously connect:

- one institution;
- an old Evidence record;
- another actor;
- an Agenda;
- an obligation;
- a later hearing;
- an Eastbank fragment.

This is preferable to many isolated high-word-count characters or lore entries.

### 6.41 Round 8 decision

Approved:

- authored causal depth plus generated surface breadth;
- focused regional launch footprint;
- approximately 25–35 important persistent locations;
- existing institutional network rather than faction proliferation;
- common institution authoring contract;
- approximately 12–16 Tier A and 30–50 Tier B launch actors;
- Tier C population generated and promotable;
- approximately 6–8 Signature Cases;
- approximately 10–20 reusable Institutional Case Patterns;
- emergent Matters from live simulation;
- causal Case Skeletons rather than scene scripts;
- approximately 8–12 Anomaly Families;
- approximately 16–24 Starter Claim Seeds and 30–40 established archetypes;
- reusable Claim effect primitives;
- fragmented Eastbank Canon;
- four-layer Eastbank revelation;
- authored content may remain undiscovered;
- replay through route / information / Settlement divergence rather than randomized core Truth;
- modular content resources;
- explicit hard-authored / bounded-model-assisted / freely-generative boundary;
- interconnection density as the preferred content-quality metric.

Rejected:

- preauthoring the entire city population and ordinary location inventory;
- adding fantasy factions merely for quantity;
- dozens of Tier A autonomous actors at launch;
- linear scene-script Case architecture;
- one monster entry per anomaly presentation;
- a 100+ Claim launch catalog without systemic need;
- one giant Eastbank lore file;
- forcing every authored lore fragment into every campaign;
- random culprit substitution as the main replay system;
- one giant always-loaded Package content blob.

### 6.42 Round 8.5 question — Signature Case roles and long-form mystery structure

Round 8.5 must assign distinct design responsibilities to the 6–8 Signature Cases before any detailed plot scripting begins.

It must determine:

- what gameplay system each Signature Case introduces or stress-tests;
- which institution(s) it foregrounds;
- which Principle / anomaly family it explores;
- which part of city life it reveals;
- what Eastbank / Reality Consolidation information it may expose;
- what kind of Settlement choice it creates;
- what persistent consequence can survive Case closure;
- how Cases may be encountered in different orders without breaking revelation logic;
- which Cases are mandatory for the opening / first Breach and which are discoverable / optional;
- how the long mystery emerges from shared Evidence / institutions rather than a rigid chapter sequence.
